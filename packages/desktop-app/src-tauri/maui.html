🌺 Maui Family Vacation Guide 🌺

August 8-13, 2026 | Lahaina, Hawaii

🏖️ Recommended Family Activities

🏖️ Best Beaches for Young Kids (Near Lahaina)

  • Baby Beach (Pu'unoa Beach) - The #1 recommended beach for families with young children
    • Protected by offshore reef, calm shallow waters
    • No strong currents or big waves
    • Located right in Lahaina along Front Street
  • Launiupoko Beach Park - Another excellent option with gentle waves and shallow entry points

🎯 Top Attractions & Activities

  • Maui Ocean Center - One of the best aquariums in the US
    • Perfect for all ages, great educational experience
    • World's largest collection of live Pacific corals
  • Sunset Boat Tours - Barefoot sailing with live Hawaiian music
    • Magical, mellow activity perfect for families
  • Ululani's Shave Ice - Famous Hawaii tradition (5 locations on Maui)
    • Must-do treat for kids!
  • Local Farmers Markets - Budget-friendly, authentic Hawaiian experience

📋 Sample 5-Day Itinerary

Day 1 (Aug 8): Arrive + settle in, afternoon at Baby Beach
Day 2 (Aug 9): Maui Ocean Center morning, sunset boat tour evening
Day 3 (Aug 10): Farmers market morning, beach day afternoon, shave ice
Day 4 (Aug 11): Explore Lahaina town, Launiupoko Beach Park
Day 5 (Aug 12): Relaxed beach day, favorite activities
Day 6 (Aug 13): Departure

🏄‍♂️ Surfing Lesson Costs in Maui

👥 Group Lessons (Most Budget-Friendly)

  • $88-$135 per person for group lessons (2 hours)
    • Best value: $88/person at places like Surf Shack Maui
    • Typical group size: 6-8 people with one instructor

👨‍👩‍👧 Semi-Private/Small Group Lessons

  • 2 people: ~$260 total ($130 each)
  • 3 people: ~$335 total (~$112 each)
  • 4 people: ~$410 total (~$103 each)
  • 3-6 people: $119/person (Rivers To The Sea)

⭐ Private Lessons (Best for Young Kids/Nervous Surfers)

  • 1 person: $150-$250 (2 hours)
  • 2 people: ~$125 each
  • 3+ people: ~$100 each

✅ What's Typically Included:

  • Surfboard and all gear
  • Rash guard (protective shirt)
  • Reef shoes/booties (protects feet)
  • Safety briefing & land instruction
  • In-water instruction (1.5-2 hours)
  • CPR/first aid certified instructors

💰 Budget Estimate for Family of 4:

Group Lesson ~$350-475 total
Semi-Private ~$410-480 total
Private Lesson ~$400-500 total

🏆 Popular Options Near Lahaina:

  • Maui Surfer Girls - Great reviews, family-friendly
  • Surf Shack Maui - Most affordable at $88/person
  • Sea Maui Surf - Specializes in semi-private family sessions
Recommendation:
• For young kids (ages 5-10): Consider private or semi-private lessons (~$100-130/person) for less crowded, personalized attention
• For older kids (10+): Group lessons at $88-119/person offer great value and fun social experience

🍹 Local Hawaiian Alcoholic Beverages to Try

1. Ocean Organic Vodka 🥇 Top Recommendation
🏭 Made by: Hawaii Sea Spirits Organic Farm & Distillery
📍 Location: Kula, Upcountry Maui (short drive from Lahaina)
🌊 Unique Feature: Made from organic sugarcane + deep ocean water sourced 3,000 ft below sea level
🎟️ Experience: Tours available daily 9:30am-5pm (recommend booking ahead, tiny tasting room)
💰 Price: Premium pricing but unique taste experience
2. Maui Brewing Company 🍺 Most Accessible
🏭 Hawaii's largest craft brewery
📍 Location: Restaurant in Lahaina (Kihei production facility)
🍻 Popular Beers: Hono Kölsch, Island IPA, Tropical variations with pineapple/passionfruit
✨ Best bet: Easy to find on tap at restaurants across Maui

🏪 Where to Buy Local Drinks:

  • ABC Stores throughout Lahaina - carry local beers and rums
  • Maui Liquor Stores - well-stocked with island products
  • Many resorts/restaurants serve these locally-made beverages

🍸 Recommended Orders at Restaurants:

1️⃣ Ask for Maui Brewing Company draft beer 🍺
2️⃣ Try cocktails made with Ocean Vodka or Kuleana Rum 🍹
3️⃣ Ask bartenders for locally-sourced spirit recommendations 🤙

💡 Quick Tips:

  • Lahaina Farmers Markets - Great for grabbing fresh, vegetarian breakfast/snacks
  • Call ahead for reservations at popular spots like The Wooden Crate
  • Most restaurants are happy to modify dishes to be vegetarian - just ask!
